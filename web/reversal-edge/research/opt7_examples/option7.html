Buy the Z-swing signals that come after the market turns

Option 7 keeps every part of the Z swing rule and adds one condition about the whole market: the dip buys that pay are the ones that come after the market has started to recover. Signals that fire while the whole market is still falling are skipped.

The rule, in three parts

  1. The market has fallen at least 10% from its 1-year high (it is a real sell-off).
  2. It then closes at least 4% above its lowest close of the last 20 sessions. That close is the turn.
  3. For the 20 sessions after a turn, Z-swing signals are bought as usual. Signals at other times are skipped, and a stock the normal rule bought before the turn stays blocked for its 40 sessions.

What it did, 2013-2026

Every sell-off with Z-swing trades

Option 7 helped most in long, grinding sell-offs (2020, 2022). It cost money when the market snapped back in a few days (April 2025), because most signals came before the turn, and in late 2015, when the 4% bounce was a bear-market rally.

Z swing signals: daily stretch Z first close at or below −2.62 or weekly at or below −2.08, ML rank at least 50%, market model positive, market momentum at or below −1.18, liquid stocks; bought at the next open, held 40 sessions, 0.4% costs.
