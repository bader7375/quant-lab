- Abroad, the swing passed: line -2.0, sell at stretch Z >= -0.5, one buy a day beat the 40-session ladder in 66% of 64 market-eras

- What I already know about 2020-26 from the earlier rounds' write-ups, so nobody thinks that period is blind for exits: rounds 41,
  53 and 70 report that "sell when stretch Z is back at 0" did about as well as holding there, with more winners and smaller losses.

## What the user asked for, and what can honestly be judged
A swing system: enter on the cross into the buy zone, sell high, many trades a year, low risk. Two verdicts are kept apart:
- AGAINST YOUR RULES: the strict verdict against LIVE (growth not lower, drawdown not more than 0.2 point higher, return per
  drawdown higher, in 2013-19 and in 2020-26, at equal risk), and the same numbers against the ladder.
- AS A SWING SYSTEM IN ITS OWN RIGHT ("sound"): in each of 2013-19, 2020-26 and 2023-26 it grows faster than idle money alone;
  its worst drawdown is not above LIVE's; at least 65% of trades win; and it trades more often than LIVE.
If it is sound but not confirmed against your rules, it is offered as a style choice with the cost written next to it.

## The core rule (fixed now, no further search)
Candidates were limited to the stage-A rules that were good abroad (beat the ladder in at least 59% of market-eras with a higher
median return per drawdown): line -2.0 with sell at -1, -0.5 or 0; line -2.5 with sell at -1 or 0; line -3.0 with sell at -1 or -0.5.
Among those that beat LIVE on Saudi 2013-19 (growth not lower, drawdown not higher), the one with the most trades, ties broken by
return per drawdown. That is: **line -2.5, sell at stretch Z >= 0, 40 sessions at most** (54 trades in 2013-19, +3.9% / 5.4%).
Everything else as in round 77: storm or deep weather, live gates, one buy a day (most volatile first), 12 slots of 2.1% (4.2% deep),
re-entry on a new cross.

## One optional layer for more trades (the user's wish), tested on 2013-19 before 2020-26 is opened
Quick swings outside sell-offs at half size (1.04%): cloudy weather, sell at stretch Z >= -1 or after 20 sessions; calm weather,
sell at stretch Z >= -1.5 or after 10 sessions; same -2.5 line and gates. Round 76 says such crosses have a small edge abroad
(+0.8% to +1.4% a trade) and no clear one in Saudi, and the bounce is short.
- The layer is kept only if, on Saudi 2013-19, the account with it is not worse than the core alone: growth not lower by more than
  0.1 point and worst drawdown not higher by more than 0.2 point. Tested in two steps: clouds only, then clouds and calm.
  The largest layer that meets the mark is kept; otherwise none.
